# CS2 Chicken Desktop Pet v1.1.0

A real-time 3D desktop companion for Windows, using the models, materials, and animations from your local Counter-Strike 2 installation.

## Highlights

- **More pets:** regular, Polish, and Silkie chickens with their available appearances, plus hatching and pristine eggs. Models and compatible animations are discovered at startup; missing selections fall back to the default chick.
- **Hover interactions:** feed, perform, sleep, or wake your pet. Choose supported actions such as sitting, startling, tail wagging, dancing, jumping, kicking, and flying.
- **Inspection and photo studio:** rotate, zoom, rename your pet, switch appearances, choose a background, and save PNG snapshots to a local photo library. The interface takes inspiration from CS2's pet inspection UI.
- **Compact context menu:** seven main entries, grouped interactions and settings, sleep/wake controls that reflect the current state, and checked settings toggles.
- **Project information:** author credits, the MIT license, and third-party notices are available in the About menu. The earlier 2D source remains in `Pet/`.

## Fixes and Improvements

- Restored animations for chicken variants and fixed incorrect chick lighting after switching pets.
- Preserved fine feather detail while fixing bright gaps on light backgrounds and rectangular halos on dark backgrounds.
- Added safe framing for animations, keeping the camera stable during playback to reduce clipping and apparent size changes.
- Refined the inspection layout, window dragging, zoom controls, and mouse rotation direction.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, x64.
- Counter-Strike 2 installed locally.
- [.NET 10 Desktop Runtime for Windows x64](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0).

The release contains a single EXE. Game models, materials, animations, and interaction icons are read from your local CS2 installation.

## Getting Started

1. Download `ChickenDesktopPet3D-v1.1.0-win-x64.zip` from the release assets.
2. Extract the archive and run `ChickenDesktopPet3D.exe`.
3. Allow a few seconds for the initial load.

If CS2 cannot be located automatically, set the `CHICK_CS2_VPK` environment variable to the absolute path of `pak01_dir.vpk`.

To upgrade, exit the previous version and replace the EXE. Settings remain in `%APPDATA%\ChickenDesktopPet\settings3d.json`; photos are stored under your Windows Pictures folder. Pet names and photos are local and do not connect to Steam inventory progression or photo services.

Use the attached `SHA256SUMS.txt` to verify the archive or extracted EXE.

## Controls

- Single-click to react, double-click to perform, middle-click to feed, drag to move, and scroll to rotate.
- Hover for about half a second to open the interaction bar; right-click the pet or tray icon to open the menu.
- In the inspection studio, drag the pet to rotate and scroll to zoom. Drag the title or an empty preview area to move the window.
- Available actions depend on the selected model's animations. The pristine egg is a static appearance.
